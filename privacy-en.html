Privacy Policy — Tides of Legend RPG

Last updated: 17-10-2026

This policy explains what data the game Tides of Legend RPG (formerly "Mareas de Leyenda"; Android app with the package com.alexa.mareasdeleyenda), developed by AmanteRechoncho, who is the controller of that data, processes. Contact: alexandrocov10@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The game creates an anonymous account (a random code; it does not ask for your real name, email or phone number) to save your progress in the cloud, to validate gems, summons and purchases on the server, and to run the Arena, alliances and in-game mail.
  • Data is stored on Supabase servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • There are optional real-money purchases. They are charged by Google Play: we never see your card or payment details.
  • You can choose a display name and a portrait that other players see. If you join an alliance, its members read what you write in its chat.
  • Linking your account with Google is optional.
  • You can delete your account and its data from within the game at any time (Settings → ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT AND DATA) or request it by email.

We do not ask for your location, contacts, photos or phone number. Sound, vibration and language settings are stored only on your device.

Sign in with Google (optional)

Only if you tap LINK WITH GOOGLE or SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE (Settings → ACCOUNT) do we store your Google account identifier and email, together with the name and profile picture that Google includes in the sign-in (the game does not use them or show them to anyone). They are used only to recover your account on another phone: we do not share them with anyone and we do not send advertising to that email. The game requests no other permission on your Google account. They are deleted when you delete your account. If you sign in with a Google account that has no save, the empty account that gets created is deleted straight away.

What other players see

Your display name, player code, portrait, frame and title, your defence team and its power, and your rating and positions on the leaderboards. Members of your alliance also see your rank, your activity and your chat messages. Do not put personal data in your name or in the chat.

Legal basis and retention

We process this data because it is necessary to provide the service you request by playing (saving your progress, the online modes and purchases) and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and the game fair (anti-cheat and technical logs).

It is kept for as long as your account exists, with these shorter exceptions:

  • MOVE TO NEW PHONE codes last 24 hours at most and are deleted when used.
  • Each alliance keeps only its last 100 chat messages; older ones are deleted.
  • Gift code redemption attempts only count for one minute (a limit against brute force) and are deleted on your next redemption.
  • The provider's technical logs are deleted after a few days.

When you delete your account, the server immediately deletes the account and its sessions, the profile, the save, gems and coins, summons, mail, purchases, Arena, Abyss and alliance boss data, your place in the alliance, anti-cheat flags, codes and the Google link. Only the following remain, no longer linked to your account:

  • the messages you wrote in an alliance chat, with the name you had, until newer messages replace them (the last 100 are kept) or the alliance disappears;
  • the brackets of Arena tournaments already played, with the name, portrait and team you took part with, as that tournament's history.

Daily server backups are kept for 14 days at most: within that period, deleted data disappears from them too.

Your rights

You can access, correct, take away, object to the use of, or delete your data. Your display name can be changed in the game's Profile. The fastest way to delete everything is Settings → ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT AND DATA in the game. You can also request it by email at alexandrocov10@gmail.com, stating your player code (Settings → ACCOUNT → Player); we will handle it within 30 days at most and, to protect your account, we may ask for something that proves it is yours. If you uninstall the game without deleting your account, your save remains on the server and can be recovered with a MOVE TO NEW PHONE code created beforehand, or with Google if you linked it; otherwise, you can request its deletion by email.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD: https://www.aepd.es).

Providers

  • Supabase, Inc. (database, accounts and server functions), data processor, with data hosted in the EU (region eu-central-1, Frankfurt). Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Google (Google Play and, if you use it, Sign in with Google). Google Play charges purchases under its own policy. To verify each purchase, the server sends its receipt to Google Play; the purchase carries a fingerprint (hash) of your account identifier so that nobody else can use it. Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • GitHub, Inc.: in the Android beta, the game downloads a small file from GitHub at start-up to tell you when a new version is available. No account data is sent; GitHub sees your IP address like any website does. Policy: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Google and GitHub may process data outside the EU with the safeguards required by the GDPR (for example, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

Permissions

  • Internet: to save your progress in the cloud, validate gems, summons and purchases, and play the online modes. You can keep playing offline, except for summoning, buying and the online modes.
  • Vibration: to provide haptic feedback in combat and summons. It can be turned off in Settings.
  • Google Play billing: for real-money purchases (added by Google's billing library).

The game does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 14 and live in Spain, ask your parent or guardian for permission before playing online or making purchases.

Changes

If what the game does with your data changes, we will update this policy before releasing that version and change the date above.

Contact

For any privacy questions: alexandrocov10@gmail.com
