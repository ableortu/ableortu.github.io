Delete your account — Tides of Legend RPG

You can delete your Tides of Legend RPG account (Android app com.alexa.mareasdeleyenda, by AmanteRechoncho) and all its data whenever you want.

In the game (immediately)

  1. Open the game with an internet connection.
  2. Tap the gear to open Settings and go to the ACCOUNT tab.
  3. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT AND DATA and confirm.

The account and its data are deleted from the server right away. This cannot be undone: your save and everything earned or bought are lost.

By email (if you no longer have the game)

Write to alexandrocov10@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account" and include:

  • your player code (Settings → ACCOUNT → Player, e.g. "1A2B-3C4D"), or
  • the Google email you linked the account with, writing from that email.

We will delete it within 30 days at most and reply when it is done. To protect your account, we may ask for something that proves it is yours. Anonymous accounts have no contact details: without the player code or Google we cannot tell which account is yours.

What is deleted

Everything in your account: the account and its sessions, the profile (name, portrait, frames and titles), the save, gems and coins, summons, in-game mail, purchases, Arena, Abyss and alliance boss data, your place in the alliance, your alliance chat messages and the chat notices that name you, anti-cheat flags, codes and the Google link.

In the brackets of Arena tournaments already played, your name, portrait, frame and title are removed: your spot remains as an anonymous "Captain" so that the bracket stays complete. Server backups are deleted within 14 days at most.
