Tides of Legend RPG

Turn-based hero RPG for Android. Tides of Legend RPG is a turn-based pixel-art RPG for Android. Gather a crew of legendary heroes inspired by the myths of the sea and the north, summon them with the Compass of the Tides, evolve them and sail through worlds full of bosses.

  • Fast turn-based battles: elemental advantages, team Sync and Ultimate Arts with their own animations.
  • Story worlds on Normal, Hard and Nightmare, a Tower of bosses, dungeons, events and fishing.
  • PvP Arena, a weekly Abyss and alliances with chat and a shared boss every week.
  • Cloud save: carry on on another phone with a code or your Google account.
  • In English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese. No ads.
