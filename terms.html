4. Fair play

Cheating is forbidden: modifying the game or your save, using third-party programs, deliberately exploiting bugs, automating play or attacking the servers. The server checks saves and battles. We may suspend or delete cheating accounts and remove what was obtained with them. If you find a bug, please tell us.

5. What you write

Other players see your display name, team and alliance names and the alliance chat. Do not write anything offensive, insulting, discriminatory, sexual, violent or illegal; do not harass anyone, spam, advertise or scam, do not sell accounts or cheats, do not impersonate other people or share personal data (yours or anyone else's). We may change names and descriptions and delete messages that break these rules, remove players from their alliance and suspend accounts that keep doing it. Before writing in the alliance chat, setting your name or creating an alliance you must accept the community rules. You can report any chat message, any player (by tapping them) and any alliance (we review it), and block a player so you don't see their messages. What you write remains yours, but you allow us to show it inside the game.

6. Sanctions and complaints

  • What the server decides on its own: every team you use against the alliance boss, in Arena tournaments and in TORTUGA (island raids and defence) is checked against what is possible in the game. If it is impossible (level, evolution, Awakening or gear above the maximum, or a hero the server never gave you), the server suspends the account straight away, without a person being involved.
  • What a person reviews: reports from players, notices of illegal content sent to us by email (anyone can send them, whether they play or not) and anti-cheat flags that are not certain. Nobody is sanctioned because of them until the game team has looked at them.
  • Measures, depending on what happened and whether it is repeated: deleting messages, changing names or descriptions, removing players from their alliance, removing what was obtained by cheating (including the damage your account added for your alliance against that week's alliance boss), leaving the account out of leaderboards, tournaments and their prizes, and suspending it for a while or for good.
  • Suspended account: the game tells you when you connect. It cannot use anything that goes through the server (cloud save, online modes, in-game mail or purchases; a payment that cannot be delivered is refunded by Google Play), but you can still delete it from Settings → ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT AND DATA, and appeal with REPORT A PROBLEM (see "Complaints").
  • Complaints: if you think it is a mistake, send us your complaint with REPORT A PROBLEM (in Settings → INFO and in the suspended-account notice itself; it works even while the account is suspended and already includes your player code) or write to us at alexandrocov10@gmail.com with your player code (Settings → ACCOUNT → Player). A person reviews it and we reply within 30 days at most; if we got it wrong, we lift the suspension. This does not affect your right to go to court (section 9).
